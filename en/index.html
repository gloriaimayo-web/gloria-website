The Problem

Is your business glued to the phone?

Requests arrive mixed between calls and messages, schedules clash, changes get forgotten and client information ends up scattered. While you serve one client, you can't answer the next — and every unanswered message can be a lost booking.

The Result

An operation that stays organized while you serve clients

Imagine a business where clients request appointments from a link or a QR code, your calendar is organized in one place, and each client's information is saved so you can invite them back. That's what we leave up and running.

Real, Working Features

Features available (by plan)

  • A booking page personalized for your business, with its own link
  • Email appointment confirmations
  • Automatic email reminders to reduce no-shows
  • A client record (mini-CRM) that grows with every appointment
  • A QR code for your door or counter
  • Multi-professional booking, with no clashes
  • Activity reports: appointments, top services, cancellations and no-shows
  • Secure-link cancellation so clients can free up a slot
